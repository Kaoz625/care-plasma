To Donate

Everything you need
to get started

Donating plasma is safe, simple, and rewarding. Here's a complete guide to eligibility, preparation, and what to expect on donation day.

Eligibility

Do you qualify?

Most healthy adults can donate. Check these basic requirements before booking.

🎂

Age

Donors must be between 18 and 69 years old.

⚖️

Weight

A minimum weight of 110 lbs (50 kg) is required.

❤️

General Health

Must be in good health with no active infections on the day of donation.

🪴

Valid ID

A government-issued photo ID and proof of address required at your first visit.

💊

Medications

Some medications may affect eligibility. Our team will review prescriptions during screening.

⏱️

Donation Interval

Wait at least 2 days between plasma donations. Up to twice per week.

The Process

What happens on
donation day

1

Check In & Registration

Arrive and check in at the front desk. First-time donors complete a health history questionnaire.

2

Health Screening

A staff member checks your blood pressure, pulse, temperature, and hemoglobin levels before every donation.

3

Plasmapheresis

A needle is gently placed in your arm. A machine draws blood, separates the plasma, and safely returns the remaining components to your body.

4

Rest & Refreshments

After donating, you'll rest briefly while our team monitors you. Stay hydrated and enjoy a small snack before leaving.

5

Receive Compensation

Your payment is processed immediately after a successful donation.

Preparation Tips

Set yourself up
for success

✓
Drink plenty of water

Stay well-hydrated the day before and morning of your donation.

✓
Eat a protein-rich meal

Have a nutritious meal within 3 hours of donating. Avoid fatty foods right before.

✓
Get enough sleep

A good night's rest helps your body perform well during and recover after donation.

✓
Avoid alcohol

Skip alcohol for at least 24 hours before donating.

✓
Wear comfortable clothing

Wear short sleeves or loose-fitting clothes to allow easy access to your arm.

✓
Bring your ID

Government-issued photo ID and proof of address for first-time visits.
