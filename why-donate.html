Who You Help

Lives touched by
every donation

🩺

Hemophilia Patients

Regular infusions of plasma-derived clotting factors allow people with hemophilia to live normal lives.

🛡️

Immune Deficiencies

Patients with immunodeficiency diseases rely on immunoglobulin therapies — made from pooled plasma — to fight off infections.

🔥

Burn & Trauma Victims

Albumin, a plasma protein, is critical for treating severe burns and traumatic injuries.

🧬

Rare Disease Patients

Conditions like alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency are treated exclusively with plasma-derived therapies.

🤰

Rh Disease Prevention

Rh immunoglobulin protects thousands of babies from Rh disease every year.

🔬

Medical Research

Plasma donations fuel research into new therapies, vaccines, and treatments for future generations.

Know the Difference

Plasma vs. whole blood

Plasma Donation

  • ✅Red blood cells returned — less fatigue
  • ✅Donate up to twice per week
  • ✅Plasma replenishes within 24–48 hours
  • ✅Compensated — earn $50–$60 per session
  • ✅Treats 100+ rare and chronic conditions

Whole Blood Donation

  • ℹ️All blood components removed — longer recovery
  • ℹ️Can only donate every 56 days
  • ℹ️Red cells take 4–6 weeks to replenish
  • ℹ️Typically not compensated
  • ℹ️Primarily used for surgeries and emergencies
