🔬 Basics
What is plasma donation? +
Plasma donation involves giving a portion of your blood used to help patients with various medical conditions. Plasma is the liquid part of blood containing proteins vital for clotting and immunity. It cannot be manufactured — it must come from human donors.
How is plasma different from whole blood? +
When you donate plasma, blood is drawn, plasma is separated using a plasmapheresis machine, and the remaining components are safely returned to your body. This is why you can donate plasma more frequently than whole blood.
What is plasma used for? +
Plasma is used to create life-saving therapies for hemophilia, immune deficiencies, severe burns, rare diseases, and more. Over 600 medicines worldwide are derived from donated plasma.
✅ Eligibility
Who can donate plasma? +
Most healthy adults between 18 and 69 years old who weigh at least 110 lbs (50 kg) can donate. You must be in good general health with no active infections.
How often can I donate? +
You may donate plasma up to twice per week, with at least 2 days between donations. Your plasma replenishes within 24–48 hours.
Can I donate if I take medications? +
Some medications may affect eligibility. Our staff will review your current prescriptions during the screening process. Always disclose all medications.
⚗️ The Process
How long does the donation process take? +
A typical session takes about 90 minutes. First-time donors should plan for approximately 2 hours due to additional intake paperwork and orientation.
Does donating plasma hurt? +
Most donors experience minimal discomfort — only a brief pinch when the needle is inserted. The rest of the process is painless.
What should I bring? +
Bring a government-issued photo ID and proof of address for your first visit. Wear comfortable, short-sleeved clothing and stay well-hydrated.
🛡️ Safety
Is plasma donation safe? +
Yes. Plasma donation is safe and well-regulated. Care Plasma adheres to strict safety, sanitation, and hygiene protocols. All equipment is sterile and single-use.
Are there any side effects? +
Some donors experience mild lightheadedness or slight bruising at the needle site. Staying hydrated before and after significantly reduces the likelihood of side effects.
💰 Compensation
Do donors receive compensation? +
Yes. Each successful full donation pays $50–$60, with bonuses through our referral program. Regular donors can earn up to $500 per month.
How does the referral program work? +
Refer a friend and earn $10 for each friend who completes a donation. No cap — refer as many people as you like.
🌿 Aftercare
What should I do after donating? +
Drink plenty of fluids, eat a nutritious meal, and avoid strenuous exercise for the rest of the day. Keep the bandage on for at least 4 hours.
How quickly does plasma replenish? +
Your plasma replenishes within 24–48 hours, which is why you can safely donate up to twice per week.
